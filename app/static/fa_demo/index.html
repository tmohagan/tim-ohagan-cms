Diagnostic 01 • Value & Price Architecture

Shifting from “What You Pay” to “What You Get”

Nobody wakes up thinking about “price architecture.” They just want to feel like what they bought was worth it—and worth it doesn’t necessarily mean the cheapest thing on the menu or shelf.

The Brand-Side Lens

How do we elevate perceived quality, occasion fit, emotional satisfaction, and brand equity so value feels generous rather than discounted?

The Buyer / P&L Lens

How do pack-price architecture, promotional guardrails, and penny profit work for both the brand P&L and the retailer’s category margin?

The Franca Unlock

Reframe the value equation around “What makes this feel worth it?”—aligning product experience, pack/tier architecture, and messaging to protect margin while driving traffic and velocity.
